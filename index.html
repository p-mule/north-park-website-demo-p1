FAQs

Answers to the questions parents ask us most often in Lusaka.

Contact

How do we apply?

Visit our How to Apply page and complete the form. Our admissions office will call you to schedule a visit. Bring your child’s previous school records if they have them.

What are the school hours?

Classes run from 7:30 in the morning to 4:00 in the afternoon. Baby class finishes earlier at noon. Tuition activities run until 5:00.

Where do we buy uniforms?

The green and white uniform is sold at the school office. We keep all sizes in stock from baby class through twelfth grade. Bring your child for a fitting.

What activities do you offer?

Football, netball, chess, and a school choir meet after classes. Older students can join the debate club. All activities are included in tuition.

When does the school year start?

The Zambian school year begins in January and ends in December. We accept new students at the start of each term. Mid-term admission is possible if space allows.
